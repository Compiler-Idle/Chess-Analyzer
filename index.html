Every label below is taken from the engine's own report for that position: the centipawn (or mate) score before the move, the score of the position after the played move, and the engine's alternative lines. All numbers are from the mover's point of view. Win probability uses the standard conversion 50 + 50·(2/(1+e^(−0.00368208·cp)) − 1), and a forced mate is smoothed to 100 − 2·(moves−1)% so a mate in 5 is not treated as an instant 100% swing.
Rules are applied top to bottom and the first match wins.
Accuracy score. Each counted move gets a per-move accuracy 103.1668·e^(−0.04354·winLoss) − 3.1669 from its own win% loss. The headline game accuracy is a weighted average of those numbers, not a plain one: each move is weighted by the local standard deviation of win% in a small window around it (window size clamp(plies/10, 2, 8), weight itself clamped to 0.5–12), so a slip deep in an already-decided position counts for less than one in a sharp, contested position. The plain (unweighted) average is still shown in the Engine metrics table for comparison.
